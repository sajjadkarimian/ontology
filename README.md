# Ontologies by Sajjad Karimian
 
These ontologies come from my MSc by Research in Computer Science at University College Dublin. They describe how data moves through clinical work systems: who records it, who uses it, and who is accountable for it.

## Atlas Ontology
 
Atlas extends Socio-Technical Systems Analysis (STSA) with a machine-readable way to describe data flows, data use and accountability in complex work systems. Existing STSA methods record activities and actors, but treat data implicitly. Atlas makes it explicit, so data can be traced across organisational and technical boundaries.

## SEIPS Work System Ontology
 
A formal model of healthcare work systems based on SEIPS (Systems Engineering Initiative for Patient Safety): people, tasks, tools, organisation and environment. It supports analysis of how data use and AI adoption fit into clinical work.
 
- **Documentation:** [sjka.site/ontology/seips/](https://www.sjka.site/ontology/seips/)
- **Download:** [Turtle (.ttl)](https://www.sjka.site/ontology/seips/ontology.ttl) · [OWL](https://www.sjka.site/ontology/seips/ontology.owl) · [JSON-LD](https://www.sjka.site/ontology/seips/ontology.jsonld) · [N-Triples](https://www.sjka.site/ontology/seips/ontology.nt)
## IPC Data Transparency Knowledge Graph
 
A FAIR-aligned knowledge graph that applies Atlas and SEIPS to an Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) workflow, showing data flows, provenance and responsibilities.
