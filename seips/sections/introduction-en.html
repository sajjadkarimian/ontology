Introduction back to ToC

Health information systems and AI tools are often evaluated on technical performance alone, while the work system they are placed into decides whether they are adopted and used safely. The SEIPS model describes that work system through five interacting elements: the person, the tasks they perform, the technology and tools they use, the organisation, and the physical environment. This ontology turns the SEIPS work system into classes and object properties (for example performsTask, usesTechnologyAndTools, responsibleForTask and worksInEnvironment), so that a clinical work system can be described as linked data, queried and compared. It was developed as part of an MSc by Research at University College Dublin and is applied, together with the Atlas ontology, in the IPC Data Transparency Knowledge Graph.
