Abstract

The SEIPS Work System Ontology is a formal, machine-readable model of the healthcare work system described by the Systems Engineering Initiative for Patient Safety (SEIPS) model (Carayon et al., 2006). It represents the five work system elements, Person, Task, Technology and Tools, Organisation and Environment, and the relationships between them. The ontology supports structured analysis of how people, tasks and technology interact in clinical settings, and is designed to be used together with the Atlas ontology to trace data flows and accountability within those work systems.
